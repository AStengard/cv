"SECTION 12. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL 12.1 General Prohibition. Neither party may assign, delegate, or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, whether voluntarily, involuntarily, by operation of law, or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other party. 12.2 Change of Control of Provider. Any Change of Control of Provider shall be deemed an assignment requiring Client's prior written consent, which consent may be granted or withheld in Client's sole and absolute discretion. For purposes of this Agreement, "Change of Control" means: (a) any consolidation, amalgamation, or merger of Provider with or into any other entity; (b) any transaction or series of related transactions in which more than fifty percent (50%) of Provider's voting securities are transferred to an unrelated third party; or (c) any sale, lease, exchange, or other transfer of all or substantially all of Provider's assets. 12.3 Remedies for Unauthorized Change of Control. If Provider undergoes a Change of Control without obtaining Client's prior written consent at least thirty (30) days prior to the effective date of such Change of Control: (i) such transaction shall constitute an incurable material breach of this Agreement; (ii) Client shall have the right to immediately terminate this Agreement and all active SOWs upon written notice; and (iii) Provider shall immediately refund to Client all unearned prepaid fees and transition all work product without additional fee."
